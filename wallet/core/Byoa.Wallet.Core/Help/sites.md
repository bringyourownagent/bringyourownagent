# Sites and paused access

The Sites page shows every site that asked, your answer, its skills, and its recent activity. Allowing a site does not give it unrestricted access: its grant, language, purpose, and limits are checked on every call.

A site that works with several models (a judge that wants different AIs) asks for them in its request: it says how many it needs, which it requires or only recommends, and why. The consent window ticks what you have, you change the ticks and may add models from your own keys, and the site gets only the models you ticked, by a code of its own; it never sees the others, and sees their names only if it asked and you allow. Its card in Sites keeps the list: add, remove and reorder models, hide the names; a change reaches the site at once.

You can pause, revoke, block, or unblock a site. A paused site keeps its saved choice but its old sessions stop. Press Resume on its card to lift the pause (the site connects again with its next request), or let its next request, after your click, ask you to resume. An agent cannot allow a site at all; only you can, in the site's consent window. With Full access it can also pause, resume, revoke, block or unblock a site.

A site may also say what its models must be able to do: read pictures, draw pictures, hear audio, or that it wants your own agent. The window says it in a line of short words under the site's reason. Models that fit are ticked first; a model whose abilities the wallet does not know is in the list, marked "not known", and never ticked for you; a model that falls short is left out and counted ("2 of your models do not fit", with the reason for each), unless the site asked to see such models, and then it is shown with what it lacks and still never ticked. A model is offered to a site in every section whose output it produces: one that writes text is offered for text, also when it draws pictures as well, and one that only draws is never offered for text. Its card in Sites shows the same marks, and the site is told, for each model it is given, a yes or no for each thing it asked about, and never the numbers.

A site may ask for pictures as well as text (Image generation, often Optional). Its request then has a second section of models to tick, with the same marks, search and “N of your models do not fit” as the text section, and its own list on the site's card in Sites. A model that writes text and draws pictures is in both sections and you tick it in each one where you want it; a model that only draws is only in the picture section; your agent never draws. Pictures are drawn only by models behind your OpenRouter key, and a picture costs money that the wallet cannot know in advance (OpenRouter's list gives a price per word, not per picture; only a model it lists as free is known to be free): the wallet asks before the first one, as it does before any paid call of unknown price, and counts what OpenRouter says it cost against the site's budget and your limits. A picture bigger than the site needs is made smaller before it is sent, in its own type; one that cannot be made to fit is dropped. Untick an optional picture section and the site gets no pictures.
