# Sites and paused access

The Sites page shows every site that asked, your answer, its skills, and its recent activity. Allowing a site does not give it unrestricted access: its grant, language, purpose, and limits are checked on every call.

You can pause, revoke, block, or unblock a site. A paused site keeps its saved choice but its old sessions stop and the next request needs your click and a resume decision. An agent cannot allow a site at all; only you can, in the site's consent window. With Full access it can also pause, resume, revoke, block or unblock a site.
