# Sites and paused access

The Sites page shows every site that asked, your answer, its skills, and its recent activity. Allowing a site does not give it unrestricted access: its grant, language, purpose, and limits are checked on every call.

You can pause, revoke, block, or unblock a site. A paused site keeps its saved choice but its old sessions stop. Press Resume on its card to lift the pause (the site connects again with its next request), or let its next request, after your click, ask you to resume. An agent cannot allow a site at all; only you can, in the site's consent window. With Full access it can also pause, resume, revoke, block or unblock a site.
