# Limits and money

The wallet applies all limits together: a site's grant, calls and input size, a source's paid cost, the site's daily budget, and the whole-wallet daily budget. The tightest limit wins. Local calls cost zero money but can still be limited by the site or by the wallet.

When a paid fallback is needed, the wallet says what it should cost and asks you unless the site's setting allows it. Raising a limit in the wallet affects future calls; through the wallet, an AI agent cannot raise it unless you gave that agent Full access.
