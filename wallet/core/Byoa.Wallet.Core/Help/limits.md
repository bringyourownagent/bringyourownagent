# Limits and money

The wallet applies all limits together: a site's grant, calls and input size, a source's paid cost, the site's daily budget, and the whole-wallet daily budget. The tightest limit wins. Local calls cost zero money but can still be limited by the site or by the wallet.

For text, four limits say how much room a site has: the characters it may send in one call (100,000 to start), the tokens one answer may have (20,000), how long the wallet waits for one answer (300 seconds, a model that is still starting included) and how many questions are in flight at once (8). The Limits page sets them for every site, and again for one site. A site may ask for more in its request: the consent window says what, and a tick (on) accepts it; the site's card in Limits keeps what it recommended beside your own values, with a button to use them. A site can only ever be held back by what it declared itself. The wallet has ceilings of its own that no setting passes.

When a paid fallback is needed, the wallet says what it should cost and asks you unless the site's setting allows it. Raising a limit in the wallet affects future calls; through the wallet, an AI agent cannot raise it unless you gave that agent Full access.
